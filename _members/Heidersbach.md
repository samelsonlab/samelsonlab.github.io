description: "I grew up in the SF Bay Area and moved to LA to attend UCLA, where I graduated in 2025 with a BS in Biochemistry and a minor in History. I participated in the UCLA Departmental Scholars Program, graduating in 2026 with an MS in Biochemistry, Molecular and Structural Biology (BMSB). During my Master&#39;s degree, I worked in the lab of Dr. Joseph Loo, using native and top-down mass spectrometry to study the small oligomers formed by the amyloid proteins α-synuclein and islet amyloid polypeptide, as well as their interactions with homologous proteins. I am excited to now be a PhD student in BMSB. As a rotation student in the Samelson lab, I am using ion mobility and crosslinking mass spectrometry to investigate the conformational ensembles of tau and α-synuclein monomers in vitro and from iPSC-derived neurons. Outside of the lab, I enjoy swimming, hiking, reading, and ballet."
